Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to technical, commercial, legal, and operational questions regarding construction, flat renovation, building demolition, and society redevelopment.

A turnkey 3BHK flat renovation (covering plumbing replacement, electrical rewiring, bathroom dismantling, waterproofing, new flooring, false ceiling, and modular kitchen) typically takes between 45 to 65 working days depending on the structural condition and society working-hour regulations.

Complete building demolition requires a Municipal Demolition Permit, Disconnection Certificates from Electricity and Water Supply Boards, and a structured Debris Disposal Plan. We manage the entire municipal liaison and documentation process.

We execute projects exclusively on an itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ). Every material brand (e.g., UltraTech 53-Grade cement, Tata Tiscon Fe550D TMT, Astral CPVC, Finolex wiring) and unit rate is defined prior to contract signing, preventing mid-project cost escalations.

For NRI clients living overseas, our senior engineers act as independent third-party site auditors. We provide weekly high-resolution 4K video logs, material lab test certificates, stage payment sign-offs, and conduct live WhatsApp/Zoom video walk-throughs.
