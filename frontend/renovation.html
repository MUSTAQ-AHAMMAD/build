SPECIALIZED RESTORATION SCOPES

Targeted Solutions for Every Flat Condition

01

Old Resale Flat Renovation

Full demolition of hollow brick partition walls, re-routing rusted GI plumbing with CPVC/PPR pipes, electrical distribution box rewiring, and open living room expansions.

02

Damaged Flat Structural Repair

Chipping deteriorated concrete, applying zinc-rich anti-corrosion primer on exposed rebar, polymer-modified mortar (PMM) jacketing, and PU waterproofing.

03

Rental & High-Yield Upgrades

High-durability vitrified tile flooring, modular quartz kitchen countertops, anti-skid bathroom renovations, and modern concealed lighting designed to maximize rental return.

04

Kitchen & Bathroom Overhauls

Sunken slab waterproofing barrier treatments, wall-hung sanitary ware, diverter mixers, modern ventilation exhaust shafts, and ergonomic storage.

05

Interior Civil & Architectural Works

Acoustic false ceiling gypsum boards, UPVC double-glazed soundproof sliding windows, flush doors with designer laminates, and acrylic emulsion painting.

06

Apartment Common Areas

Staircase granite cladding, lift lobby aesthetic upgrades, external weather-proof elastomeric painting, and terrace elastomeric waterproofing.
