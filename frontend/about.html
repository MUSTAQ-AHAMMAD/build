ABOUT BUILD+ CONSULTANCY

Engineered for Trust, Built for Longevity

We are a professional engineering and construction management firm dedicated to elevating residential and commercial property assets through meticulous planning, transparent billing, and rigorous execution.

OUR STORY

Bridging the Gap Between Engineering & Execution

Founded by chartered civil engineers, BUILD+ was established to solve the pervasive challenges in the construction industry: arbitrary contractor pricing, unverified material adulteration, unexplained project delays, and lack of accountability in complex renovation projects.

Today, our integrated team handles everything from vacant land development and structural construction to 20-year-old flat rehabilitation, controlled structural demolition, and multi-unit housing society redevelopments.
