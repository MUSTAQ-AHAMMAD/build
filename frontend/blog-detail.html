The Complete Engineering Checklist for Renovating a 20+ Year Old Resale Flat

✍️ By Vikram Reddy, Senior Structural Engineer • 🗓️ Updated September 2026 • ⏱️ 6 min read

Buying an older resale flat often offers unbeatable prime locations and spacious carpet areas that modern projects cannot match. However, properties built before 2005 harbor critical hidden infrastructure issues that require structural engineering expertise before laying cosmetic tiles.

1. Replacing Galvanized Iron (GI) Plumbing

Flats constructed in the late 1990s and early 2000s universally used GI piping. Over 20 years, internal mineral scaling reduces water pressure by up to 70%, and joint rust causes catastrophic hidden wall leakage.

Recommended Engineering Action: Replace all concealed water lines with multi-layer CPVC (for hot/cold supply) and SDR-grade PVC with rubber ring joints for drainage.

2. Electrical Load & Distribution Overhaul

Older flats were wired for 2–3 kW loads (fans and tube lights). Today, a standard 3BHK requires 8–12 kW to support multiple 2-ton inverter air conditioners, induction cooktops, dishwashers, and home office servers.

3. Sunken Slab Waterproofing Barrier

Never tile over existing bathroom floors without breaking down to the structural concrete slab. Treat micro-cracks with polymer-modified mortar and apply a 2-coat polyurethane membrane with 48-hour flood ponding tests.
