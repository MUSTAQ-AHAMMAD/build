QUALITY ASSURANCE WORKFLOW

Our 6-Stage Construction Quality Control

1. Soil & SBC Testing: Geotechnical bore-hole soil analysis to calculate precise foundation footing depth and raft reinforcement.
2. Structural STAAD Pro Modeling: Seismic Zone-compliant structural CAD analysis ensuring optimum steel bar diameter distribution.
3. Standard 53-Grade Cement & Fe550D TMT: Factory-certified raw materials with batch test reports shared with the client.
4. 21-Day Concrete Curing Protocols: Monitored chemical and ponding curing to attain maximum concrete compressive strength.
