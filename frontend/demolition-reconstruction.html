DEMOLITION & RECONSTRUCTION SCOPES

Engineered for Safety, Speed & Zero Collateral Damage

01

Complete Building Demolition

Hydraulic excavator breaker attachments, acoustic dust curtains, water mist cannons, and municipal demolition permit management for multi-story structures.

02

Partial & Internal Strip-Outs

Precision diamond wire wall sawing, non-structural brick removal, and floor slab modification without compromising existing structural RCC columns.

03

Reconstruction Post-Demolition

Immediate site leveling, soil compaction testing, new foundation footings, and accelerated RCC casting to transition seamlessly from demolition to new building.
