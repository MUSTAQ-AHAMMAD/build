STRUCTURED PROJECT LIFECYCLE

Our 10-Stage Quality-Driven Process

We eliminate guesswork, cost escalation, and quality compromises by executing every construction, renovation, demolition, and redevelopment project through a standardized 10-step milestone roadmap.

STAGE 01 01

Requirement Discussion

We meet in person or via video call to document your functional goals, budget parameters, and architectural vision.

STAGE 02 02

On-Site Technical Visit

A qualified civil engineer visits the site to survey plot boundaries, soil condition, existing RCC framing, and utility connections.

STAGE 03 03

Engineering Assessment

We run structural calculations, test concrete rebound numbers, and determine precise renovation or foundation requirements.

STAGE 04 04

Transparent BOQ Estimate

You receive a line-by-line itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with specific material brands, unit rates, and zero hidden clauses.

STAGE 05 05

Architectural & Structural Planning

2D working blueprints, 3D elevation renders, structural STAAD-Pro models, and MEP routing diagrams are finalized.

STAGE 06 06

Municipal Sanctions & Approvals

We assist in municipal building permits, society NOCs, water/electricity clearances, and regulatory compliance.

STAGE 07 07

Milestone-Based Execution

Work commences under continuous on-site civil engineer supervision with stage-linked payment releases.

STAGE 08 08

Rigorous Quality Inspection

Pressure testing of plumbing lines, megger electrical insulation tests, flood testing for waterproofing, and level checks.

STAGE 09 09

Snagging & Deep Cleaning

Comprehensive 120-point snag list resolution followed by mechanized post-construction deep cleaning.

STAGE 10 10

Formal Handover & Warranty

As-built drawings, appliance warranty certificates, structural guarantee documents, and keys handed over formally.
