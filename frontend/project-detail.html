EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Project Overview

The client purchased an aged 2,400 sq.ft 3BHK flat built in 2002. The structure exhibited severe galvanized iron (GI) plumbing decay, salt efflorescence on perimeter walls, and an outdated cramped kitchen with closed compartmentalized rooms.

Engineering Scope of Work

  • Plumbing Modernization: Completely replaced 24-year GI pipes with multi-layer CPVC hot/cold lines and soundproof SWR drainage shafts.
  • Structural Seepage Remediation: Chipped deteriorated plaster to bare brickwork, injected polyurethane grout, and applied 2-coat cementitious waterproof membranes.
  • Architectural Layout Expansion: Demolished non-load-bearing brick partitions between kitchen and dining to create a 35-foot open-plan living and culinary lounge.
  • Electrical Distribution Overhaul: Installed 3-phase DB with dedicated RCBO breakers for 4 high-tonnage VRV air-conditioners.

Key Challenges & Engineering Solutions

Challenge 1: Active Bathroom Sunken Slab Seepage

Solution: Stripped flooring down to structural RCC slab, repaired micro-cracks with polymer epoxy mortar, and laid elastomeric polyurethane water-stop barriers before casting new lightweight screed.
