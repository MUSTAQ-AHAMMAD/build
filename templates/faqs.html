Construction timelines vary by project size and complexity. A typical independent house (2,000–3,000 sq.ft) takes 10–14 months. Larger residential or commercial projects may require 18–36 months. Your project manager will provide a detailed milestone schedule before work begins.
A complete flat renovation for a 2BHK (900–1,200 sq.ft) typically takes 60–90 days. Larger flats or complex structural work may take 120–150 days. This includes civil, waterproofing, MEP, and finishing phases.
A BOQ (Bill of Quantities) is a detailed breakdown of every material, quantity, grade, and cost in your project. Unlike vague lump-sum quotes, a BOQ ensures complete transparency — you know exactly what you're paying for, and there are no mid-project surprises or price escalations.
We provide a dedicated NRI coordination service that includes regular photo and video progress updates, online Client Portal access, video call check-ins, and digital document management. You can track your property's progress from anywhere in the world, in real time.
Construction costs depend on location, specification grade, and design complexity. As an indicative range for Hyderabad and Bengaluru: Standard grade ₹1,800–2,200/sq.ft; Premium grade ₹2,500–3,200/sq.ft; Luxury grade ₹3,500+/sq.ft. Our estimate includes civil, structure, MEP, waterproofing, and basic finishing. Use our Budget Calculator for a more detailed indicative estimate.
Payments are milestone-linked. You pay in stages — advance, foundation, structure, slab, MEP, finishing, and final payment on handover. No stage payment is released without the corresponding milestone being verified and documented. Your payment receipts and stage certificates are accessible in your Client Portal.
