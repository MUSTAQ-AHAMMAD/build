Our Process

A Structured Process From First
Conversation to Final Handover.

Every BUILD+ project follows the same rigorous, transparent 10-step framework — from the moment you reach out to the day we hand over the keys.

01

Consultation

Our first conversation is about understanding your goals. We listen to your vision, timeline, constraints and expectations — no technical jargon, no pressure. Just clarity.

Free of charge Phone, video or in-person NRI-friendly remote option
02

Requirement Analysis

We document your property details, scope of work, budget range, and timeline in detail. This forms the foundation of your project brief and ensures nothing is missed.

Structured requirement capture Budget alignment Scope clarity
03

Site Inspection

Our licensed civil engineer conducts a thorough on-site assessment — measuring existing conditions, identifying structural concerns, evaluating soil or foundation requirements, and validating the scope.

Licensed engineer assigned Structural assessment Detailed site report
04

Estimate Preparation

Based on the site inspection, we prepare a 100% itemized BOQ (Bill of Quantities). Every material, grade, quantity and cost is transparent. No hidden markups. No vague lump sums.

100% itemized BOQ Material grades specified No hidden costs
05

Proposal

A formal project proposal is presented covering the full scope, commercial terms, execution timeline, milestone payment schedule, and quality guarantees.

Detailed scope document Milestone payment plan Timeline commitment
06

Approval & Planning

Once you approve the proposal, we finalize the execution schedule, assign your dedicated project manager, and mobilize the team. Government approvals and permits are handled simultaneously.

Dedicated project manager Government approvals handled Execution schedule locked
07

Project Execution

Our in-house civil team executes the project in structured phases — civil, structural, MEP, waterproofing, and finishing. Daily logs and progress photos are updated in your Client Portal.

In-house civil team Daily site updates Client Portal access
08

Quality Control

Each phase is inspected against the agreed specification before the next phase commences. No shortcut is taken. Material quality, workmanship, and finish are verified at every stage.

Stage-wise inspection Material verification Specification compliance
09

Handover

At project completion, we conduct a formal walkthrough, prepare the completion report, and hand over all relevant documentation including warranties, approvals, and as-built drawings.

Formal walkthrough Completion documentation Warranty handover
10

Post-Project Support

Our commitment doesn't end at handover. We remain available for post-project queries, warranty coordination, and any follow-up requirements — including ongoing property maintenance support.

Warranty support Maintenance coordination Ongoing client relationship
